# Candidate Bangladesh RMG export data sources

This project is designed for monthly series with stable definitions and units. Potential sources to review include:

- Export Promotion Bureau (EPB): official monthly export statistics and export performance releases.
- Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers and Exporters Association (BGMEA): monthly export-statistics summaries with knitwear/woven distinctions.
- Bangladesh Bank and Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: macroeconomic context where relevant.

Before use, record the exact source URL, retrieval date, unit (USD million or USD billion), fiscal/calendar year basis, product coverage, revisions and any changes in classification. Avoid stitching together series with inconsistent definitions without documenting the break.

The included demo CSV is synthetic and is not Bangladesh's observed export history.
